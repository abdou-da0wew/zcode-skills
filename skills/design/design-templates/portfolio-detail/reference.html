About BaiwuAbout the Studio

Baiwu was founded in the winter of Yiwei (2015), first at No. 37 Nanshan Road, Hangzhou. It all began with hesitation over a single line of text: which typeface, which paper, how many copies, and who would read it.

The director, Zhou Mobai, practiced calligraphy from childhood and spent three years learning movable-type printing at an old press in Hangzhou. We make books, we make type, and we make exhibitions about them. Slower is often better.
