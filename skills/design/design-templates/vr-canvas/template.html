About the Studio

Constructing stillness
between material
and situation.

Xubai was founded in Shanghai in 2017 — a small studio of six designers. We care about how material, situation, and time jointly shape a work before it finally lands, rather than chasing the surface of any particular style. We have worked with museums, publishers, art spaces, independent brands, and individual artists. Whatever the scale or medium, we prefer to observe first and respond second; subtract first, decorate later. Since 2020 we have kept a second work post in Kyoto, collaborating with a resident craftsperson on all material and prototyping work.
