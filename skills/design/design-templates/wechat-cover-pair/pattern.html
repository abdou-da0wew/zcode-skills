Safe Area

Bigger type·
Less ornament·
A calmer text block.

Text blockone character-cell of margin each side; title never touches the edge Line breaksbreak by word; particles and conjunctions never start or end a line Hierarchysize ≫ weight ≫ color

Layout notes — The main cover follows the Editorial Broadsheet pattern — "magazine masthead + display title + subtitle divider": Noto Serif SC Black carries the display title, Noto Sans SC Regular finishes the deck, and Fraunces with Space Grotesk breathe with the Latin text.

Titles are hand-broken by word: particles (de / zhi / suo) never open a line, conjunctions (and / or) never close one; em dashes take two character cells and are never split.
