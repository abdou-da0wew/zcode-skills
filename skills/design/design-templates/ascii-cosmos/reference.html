About
[ 01 / 05 ]

  

    

      Inkweave is a local-first intelligent thinking workbench.
      As you write, it quietly reads the context around you,
      weaving everything you have written over the past three years — every note, web excerpt, and literature annotation —
      into a bidirectionally linked graph.
      All inference runs entirely on your own computer;
      your ideas never leave the machine.
    

    

      
✦ Local-firstA quantized LLM is built in; on mainstream chips everything runs fully offline, with no dependence on any cloud API.

      
✦ Backlinked graphEvery idea is a node; the model automatically discovers latent references between them, lighting old notes back up.

      
✦ End-to-end encryptionMulti-device sync runs over peer-to-peer links; keys live only on your devices — not even we can read them.

A deeply restrained writing environment,
yet every line is quietly heard.

      
On the left, notebooks nest infinitely deep;
        in the middle sits a pure writing surface;
        on the right, a folded panel surfaces three related old notes for you.

      
No flashy icons, no interrupting pop-ups. All of the intelligence waits quietly in the corner until you summon it.

With the graph view,
see the shape of your thinking.

      
Every backlink becomes a line of gravity. The background continuously computes semantic distance between notes, pulling related ideas closer on the canvas.

      
Supports 3D force-directed layouts, free arrangement, timeline view; zooming through a hundred thousand nodes with the scroll wheel never stutters.
