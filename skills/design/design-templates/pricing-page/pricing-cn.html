What does “refundable deposit” mean?

The deposit is only placed on hold — never charged. When your batch opens, put it toward your first month, or refund it in full with one click. No reason required, no process to follow.

When does my batch open?

From Q3 2026, a batch opens roughly every eight weeks. Each batch is kept deliberately small, so the studio can answer every letter by hand.

Can I change plans after activating?

Yes. Upgrades take effect right away — we move you to the next open batch of the new plan; downgrades keep your original batch position and simply adjust the benefits.

Are the room photos I upload stored?

Photos are deleted from the server as soon as rendering completes in memory. They are never shared with third parties and never used to train any model outside your account.

Is everything in the catalog actually buyable?

Yes. Every piece comes from a designer or brand we have visited in person. If you fall for something in a render, request a “sourcing sheet” with the brand, lead time and price.
